QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is my journal private?

Entries are stored under your account in Firebase. MyJournal+ does not sell your writing. Optional AI processing only occurs when you choose to use AI Insights.

Does offline mode work?

Yes. After opening the app online, supported pages and data can be cached for offline use. Changes can synchronize when you're back online.

Do I need Premium to journal?

No. You can create an account and start journaling for free. Premium adds advanced features for users who want to go further.

How much does Premium cost?

Premium is offered as a one-time lifetime unlock for $1.50, giving access to the listed advanced Premium features.
